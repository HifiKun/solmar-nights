Solmar Nights is an original open-world crime game set in the fictional city of Solmar, built with Three.js. Explore the city on foot or by car, weave through traffic, dodge (or outrun) the police as your wanted level rises, and work through 4 story missions. A HUD and minimap keep you oriented, and synthesized sound effects bring the streets to life.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. You can also open `game/index.html` directly — no build step is needed.

## Controls

- **WASD** — move / drive
- **Mouse** — look around
- **E** — enter / exit a car
- **Click** — shoot
